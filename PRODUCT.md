# Product

## Register

product

## Audience

PaletteMe is for style-conscious people of any gender, skin tone, body size, age, and shopping preference who want fast, practical styling decisions before they wear or buy something.

Most sessions should work beautifully on mobile, while desktop should feel like a focused style workspace rather than a stretched phone screen.

## Product Purpose

PaletteMe is a personal style assistant. Color analysis is the entry point, but the durable product is a style operating system for scanning outfits, makeup, wardrobe items, and products.

The product should help users trust one clear next action:

- start quiz
- upload or capture a selfie
- scan an item
- save or correct a result
- compare with wardrobe

## Brand Personality

Modern, warm, visual, and decisive. The landing page can keep PaletteMe's soft pink identity and editorial beauty energy, but product surfaces should be calmer, clearer, and more task-first.

The interface should feel friendly without becoming childish, premium without becoming distant, and simple without becoming empty.

## UX Principles

1. One obvious next action per screen.
2. Show the visual result before the explanation.
3. Keep copy short and useful.
4. Make user corrections easy and respected.
5. Support mobile first, then give desktop a real workspace layout.
6. Never block the first result behind auth.
7. Ask for login only when saving, restoring, or using history.

## Visual Direction

- Keep DM Serif Display, DM Sans, and the existing pink landing identity.
- Keep buttons and nav text lowercase with zero letter spacing.
- Use large visual previews, compact verdict cards, and clear action groups.
- Prefer familiar app controls over decorative marketing sections.
- Use palette swatches, outfit thumbnails, and scan states as the primary visuals.

## Anti-References

- Text-heavy marketing pages.
- Generic SaaS card grids.
- Purple-blue AI gradients.
- Rigid style verdicts that reject most clothes.
- Women-only, thin-only, or makeup-required assumptions.
- Decorative motion that slows down scan, quiz, or result flows.
- Duplicate upload or camera surfaces outside the canonical quiz flow.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

PaletteMe should target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, large tap targets, reduced-motion friendliness, and inclusive copy. Makeup should be optional, clothing guidance should work for menswear, womenswear, unisex, and nonbinary styling, and AI labels must be editable.
